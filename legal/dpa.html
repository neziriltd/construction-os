1. Parties and roles

This agreement applies between the Customer (controller) and [YOUR COMPANY LTD] (processor) and forms part of the Terms. It meets the requirements of Article 28 UK GDPR.

2. Processing details

Subject matter: provision of the Construction-OS service. Duration: the term of the contract plus the deletion period. Nature and purpose: hosting, storing, displaying, exporting and (when requested) AI processing of Customer Data. Data subjects: the Customer’s staff, operatives, subcontractors, visitors and client representatives. Personal data: names, contact details, roles, signatures, photographs, site attendance and timesheets, training/competence card details, incident or observation records, employment and recruitment records (job applications, CVs, appraisals, payroll totals), right-to-work check records (date and method only – the Service is not designed to store identity document numbers), voice recordings and transcripts from the meeting recorder, and invoice, bank-transaction and CIS payment records. Special category and criminal offence data: only if the Customer records it – for example injury details in incident reports, fitness-for-work and health-surveillance outcomes, and DBS check dates and levels. The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis and an Article 9 / Schedule 1 DPA 2018 condition (and, for DBS information, an appropriate policy document), for telling workers, and for getting consent before recording meetings.
